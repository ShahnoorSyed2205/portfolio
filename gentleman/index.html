    A letter, just for you.

    Dear Pathaan,

    I grew up believing a man was someone who leaves, someone who is not there when it matters. Then you came along and quietly rewrote everything I thought I knew. You showed me that a man can be steady, kind, intelligent and present. You showed me that a man can be a whole world.

    You are the man who made me believe a true gentleman exists. Elegant without trying, sharp without announcing it, and gentle in the ways that actually count. You are the first man I loved the most, and you will always be my last. That does not need to change anything for you. It is simply true, and I wanted you to know it.

    I do not know what the future holds. We did not get our forever, and I have made my peace with that. So if this birthday turns out to be our last moment together, I want it to be a beautiful one, and I want the last thing I ever gave you to be pure admiration, with nothing asked in return.

    May every road ahead be smooth and every sky stay clear. May every engine start first time. May the people around you deserve you, and may every room you walk into feel a little warmer for it.

    Happy Birthday, with all my love and respect.

    — Syeda
